Documentation in French renders as well as documentation in English, and "On this page" is
reachable on a phone.

- Notes labelled `Remarque`, `Astuce`, `Attention`, `Avertissement` or `Mise en garde` are drawn
  as notes and warnings, like `Note`, `Tip` and `Warning`; French key names (`Entrée`, `Échap`,
  `Maj`, ...) are set as keys.
- Below 80 rem, where the right-hand rail does not fit, the table of contents is now a
  disclosure under the page summary (no script). It is a second copy of the same list, hidden
  from everyone (screen readers included) wherever the rail shows.
- Pull quotes use « guillemets » with narrow no-break spaces under `lang="fr"`.
- A short page (a search with no result, a 404) keeps the footer at the bottom of the window.
