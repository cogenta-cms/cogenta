Magazine sets an article, a text page and the search results on a centred reading axis instead of a column that left a third of the screen empty on the right. A page of running text (About, Standards) is no longer set as a news story because it has a reading time: no byline rule, no "min read", no drop cap. `cogenta serve`'s search page gives each result its summary and publication date plus a result count, and the search and form pages now carry the site's tagline, social links and footer note like every other public page.
